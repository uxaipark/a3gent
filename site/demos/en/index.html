Biosensing · Digital Twin Public demo · Research use

SOMA

A biosensing digital twin for wearable-sensor development. Place virtual sensors on a full-body anatomical model, vary physiological conditions and review the synthetic signals — so design decisions are made before any hardware is built.

  • Full-body twin — 6 body systems, 3D tissue sections at 12 skin sites
  • ECG · PPG · EEG · EMG · respiration synthesis, with CSV export
  • Sleep apnea — respiration from a sternal patch, across postures and movement
  • Ring blood-pressure monitor — PPG (red/infrared) signals and blood-pressure experiments by hand height

Dental · 3D Planning Public demo · Research use

OralPilot

An implant treatment-planning studio that unifies 3D anatomy, periodontal charting, implant placement and surgical simulation in a single workflow. Clinical data never leaves the browser.

  • DICOM · STL · X-ray input — files are processed only in the browser
  • 3D exploration of teeth, jaw and mandibular canal, plus periodontal chart
  • Rule- and geometry-based engine that drafts position, angle and size
  • Guide geometry review and step-by-step surgical simulation

Wearable Monitoring · Hospital Platform Screen demo · Load-tested

Bio-Signal IoT Platform

A hospital monitoring platform that collects vital signs from inpatients' wearable patches in real time, with alarms, location, records and EMR integration in one web interface.

  • Central monitors by ward, room and gateway; live waveforms per patient
  • Four-level alarms; patients and gateways on the hospital map
  • Sealed waveform storage with NAS/SFTP backup
  • 2,100 patients on a single small desktop computer (load test)
