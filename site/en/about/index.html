About a3gent

We build AI systems and hand them over for you to run

a3gent is an AI systems development partner. We define the problem, design, integrate and validate against your existing systems and data, and hand over a system your team can run. We do not operate the service for you: you always own operations.

What a3gent does

Build and hand over

  • Agree the problem and validation scope up front
  • Design and integrate against your systems and data
  • Validate on your own data
  • Admin tooling where needed, handed over with code and documentation
What we don't do

Run it for you

  • Operate or monitor the service on your behalf
  • Promise results for scope that has not been validated
  • Replace expert judgement, such as clinical decisions
  • Run medical-device regulatory approval

Drawing the line first lets you judge, before signing, what you take over and what stays your responsibility.

How we build

Technology is finished when it works in the field

Watching algorithms go into products and models go onto clinic screens leads to a simple conclusion: good AI is judged not by its benchmark scores but by how dependably it is used where it is placed. a3gent builds by five standards.

  1. 01
    Look at the place before the model

    First decide who makes which decision, on which screen. Even an excellent model goes unused if it is put in the wrong place.

  2. 02
    Start the design from the constraints

    Limited compute, imperfect data, busy sites. Rather than assume ideal conditions, we choose structures that keep working within the real ones.

  3. 03
    Support judgement, don't replace it

    AI should not replace experts; it should take repetition and record-keeping off their hands so they can focus on the decision.

  4. 04
    Say plainly what has not been validated

    What is validated and what is not are written side by side. Trust starts with stating exactly what a system cannot do.

  5. 05
    Build it to run without its builder

    Simple structures; code and documentation your team can take over. A good system keeps working in your hands after its builder has left.
