About a3gent

AI systems built for your team to operate

a3gent is an AI systems development company. We define the problem, design, integrate and validate against your existing systems and data, and transfer the system so your team can operate it independently. Operations remain with you.

Our role

Build and transfer

  • Problem definition and validation scope agreed up front
  • Design and integration with your existing systems and data
  • Performance validation on your data
  • Optional admin tooling; handover of source code and documentation
Out of scope

Operations and approvals

  • Service operation and ongoing monitoring
  • Guaranteed results beyond the validated scope
  • Replacing expert judgement, such as clinical decisions
  • Medical-device regulatory approval

A clear scope lets you judge, before signing, what is transferred to you and what remains your responsibility.

Development principles

Technology that works in the field

We believe good AI is judged not by benchmark scores but by its reliability in real use. a3gent develops by the following five principles.

  1. 01
    Design around the use environment

    Users, screens and decision flows are defined before a model is chosen; a model that does not fit its environment goes unused regardless of performance.

  2. 02
    Design for real constraints

    Limited compute, imperfect data and demanding workplaces are taken as given, and we choose structures that run reliably under them.

  3. 03
    Support expert judgement

    AI does not replace experts. It reduces repetitive work and record-keeping so they can focus on the decisions that matter.

  4. 04
    Transparent validation scope

    Validated and unvalidated scope are stated side by side. We believe stating limits precisely is where trust begins.

  5. 05
    Built for handover

    Simple structures and thorough documentation, so your engineering team can take the system over and maintain it.
