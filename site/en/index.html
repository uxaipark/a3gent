02 · Case studies

Problem, approach — and validation scope

Rather than a list of credentials: for each case, the problem solved, how it was solved and what is in operation — the information an adoption decision actually needs.

Clinicians, IT engineers — and now AI specialists

For medical technology and solution companies, recruiting AI specialists on top of clinical and IT experts is very hard. To take that burden off while differentiating the solution and sharpening its competitiveness, you need outside experts who work across AI and medical technology. That is the role a3gent takes on. The cases below are what that looks like.

Case 01

A test environment that validates sensor design before hardware is built

SOMA · Biosensing digital twin

Problem

Signals from biosignal-measurement hardware vary sharply with placement, posture, movement and tissue. Verifying a design means repeating experiments and building prototype after prototype, which costs a great deal of money and time and slows early design decisions.

Approach

Anatomy, sensor placement, physiological conditions and signal synthesis integrated into one virtual test environment. Researching the prototype in virtual experiments before building hardware means fewer experiments and prototypes, cutting development cost and time and raising efficiency. It also helps the team understand the medical solution, by seeing where and how the signal changes for themselves.

In operation

  • Sensor placement and synthetic signals, whole-body and local
  • Scenario screens for sleep apnea and ring PPG sensing
  • CSV export of signal data

Validation scope

A research and development environment built on synthetic signals. Clinical use requires medical-device regulatory approval after deployment. The solution becomes the customer’s own product, so the approval is obtained directly in the customer’s own name.

Case 02

Fragmented clinical data, unified into one treatment-planning workflow

OralPilot · Implant treatment-planning studio

Problem

CBCT, intraoral scans, X-rays and periodontal records sit in different tools and formats, so clinicians planning an implant case switch between tools and consolidate the information by hand.

Approach

Six stages — import, 3D exploration, periodontal exam, implant planning, guide review, surgical simulation — unified into a single on-screen workflow. Clinical files are processed only in the browser and never sent to a server, meeting data-security requirements.

In operation

  • DICOM · STL · X-ray import and 3D structure exploration
  • Placement drafts that respect clearance to key structures and neighboring teeth
  • Virtual extraction aligned to the tooth axis, and staged simulation

Validation scope

A research treatment-planning tool validated on public cases. Clinical use requires medical-device regulatory approval after deployment. The solution becomes the customer’s own product, so the approval is obtained directly in the customer’s own name.
