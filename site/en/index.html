02 · Case studies

Problem, approach — and validation scope

Rather than a list of credentials: for each case, the problem solved, the scope in operation today, and the scope not yet validated — the information an adoption decision actually needs.

Case 01

A test environment that validates sensor design before hardware is built

SOMA · Biosensing digital twin

Problem

A wearable's signal varies sharply with placement, posture, movement and tissue. Verifying that normally means building hardware and repeating human-subject trials, which makes early design decisions slow and costly.

Approach

Anatomy, sensor placement, physiological conditions and signal synthesis integrated into one test environment. R&D teams review how the signal responds first and narrow the real experiments, cutting development time and cost.

In operation

  • Sensor placement and synthetic signals, whole-body and local
  • Scenario screens for sleep apnea and ring PPG sensing
  • CSV export of signal data

Validation scope

A research environment built on synthetic signals. Blood-pressure estimation from PPG and independent validation are not implemented yet. Not for clinical decisions.

Case 02

Fragmented clinical data, unified into one treatment-planning workflow

OralPilot · Implant treatment-planning studio

Problem

CBCT, intraoral scans, X-rays and periodontal records sit in different tools and formats, so clinicians planning an implant case switch between tools and consolidate the information by hand.

Approach

Six stages — import, 3D exploration, periodontal exam, implant planning, guide review, surgical simulation — unified into a single on-screen workflow. Clinical files are processed only in the browser and never sent to a server, meeting data-security requirements.

In operation

  • DICOM · STL · X-ray import and 3D structure exploration
  • Placement drafts that respect clearance to key structures and neighboring teeth
  • Virtual extraction aligned to the tooth axis, and staged simulation

Validation scope

Rule-based geometric search, not a trained model, validated on public cases for research use. Automatic segmentation and CT–scan registration are not yet implemented; it does not estimate bone quality or judge clinical safety.

03 · How to engage

Two engagement models

Take only the scope you need. Adopting a solution is often sufficient on its own; custom development is combined when deep integration with your systems is required.

Step 1

Solution adoption

Scope of engagementA licence to a built solution, plus deployment services

  • Scope and validation criteria agreed up front

Suited when a published solution addresses most of your requirement as it stands.

Step 2

Scope of engagementDirect expert involvement to integrate, extend and validate against your systems

  • Integration with your existing systems and data
  • Features extended to fit your business processes
  • Validation designed around your own data

Rather than building every project from scratch, we accumulate reusable core solutions and extend their scope client by client. You start faster on validated operating scenarios and a proven code base, and concentrate development investment on what is unique to your business.
