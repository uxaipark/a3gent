Frequently asked questions

Can it attach to our existing devices or charting software?

Your devices and record systems stay as they are; the agent operates and records on top of them. The integration approach is agreed at kickoff.

Does it work in a noisy clinic?

It is designed around real-world noise, latency and conversational flow, and tested on site.

What about medical-device regulatory approval?

Clinical use requires medical-device regulatory approval after deployment. The solution becomes the customer’s own product, so the approval is obtained directly in the customer’s own name.

Does it handle English commands?

It can be designed to handle Korean and English commands the same way.
