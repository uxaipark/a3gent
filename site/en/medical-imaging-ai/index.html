Frequently asked questions

Which image formats can it handle?

DICOM single-frame series, NIfTI, STL, OBJ and PLY surface files, and X-ray images. Other formats are added as the problem requires.

Are patient images sent anywhere?

No. The system can be designed to process image files only in the browser, without sending them to a server.

What about medical-device regulatory approval?

Clinical use requires medical-device regulatory approval after deployment. The solution becomes the customer’s own product, so the approval is obtained directly in the customer’s own name.

Does it apply beyond dentistry?

The DICOM-based 3D exploration, reconstruction and planning flow applies to other clinical areas too. Scope is reviewed together when the problem is defined.
