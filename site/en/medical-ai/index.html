Frequently asked questions

Do we need AI staff in-house?

No. a3gent takes on problem definition, design, integration and validation, and hands over a system your own staff can run.

How do engagements work?

Either adopt a solution we have already built, or combine it with integration and extension work for your systems. Scope is set by the problem.

What about medical-device regulatory approval?

Clinical use requires medical-device regulatory approval after deployment. The solution becomes the customer’s own product, so the approval is obtained directly in the customer’s own name.

How is patient data handled?

Systems are designed to run inside your existing environment and, where needed, to process data only in the browser without sending it to a server. Security requirements are agreed before kickoff.
